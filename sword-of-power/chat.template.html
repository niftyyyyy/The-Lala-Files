Example · Why are we running the LEDs straight off the cell instead of the boost?
Prop assistant

Commercial pixel-blade sabers do the same. Running ~78 WS2812Bs off the 18650 directly means no big boost converter and no level shifter, because 3.3 V data reads reliably at a 3.4–4.2 V LED supply. The small MT3608 only feeds the ESP32-S2 and DFPlayer at 5.0 V, so a bright swing can't brown out the board mid-take.

Keep the blade capped with setMaxPowerInVoltsAndMilliamps(3.7, 1200); uncapped full white is about 4.7 A, well past the DW01's ~3 A trip.
